## General

- AI coding assistants: Claude Code (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI), used only as coding and writing assistants. No pre-trained models or AI APIs are part of the product.
- Round 1 code reused from `./reference` (engine.py, cases.json, run_cases.py, generator.py, rulepack_v0.3.json, discover.py, results/).
- All data is synthetic. No bank, customer or platform data. All names, phone numbers, UPI IDs, banks and amounts are fictional.
